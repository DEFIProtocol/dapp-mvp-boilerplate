Preamble
We, the participants of the Decentralized Commodity Settlement Network (DCSN), establish this Constitution to ensure the fair, transparent, and resilient governance of a global, open‑source, crypto‑settled commodity infrastructure.
Our purpose is to maintain an indestructible supply chain capable of operating even when traditional systems fail.
We commit to decentralization, neutrality, transparency, and the protection of essential human needs.
Article I — Mission
The mission of the DCSN DAO is to:
Govern the DCSN protocol in a decentralized, transparent manner.
Ensure the continuous movement of essential goods globally.
Maintain a censorship‑resistant, open‑source commodity settlement network.
Protect the economic resilience of communities worldwide.
Prevent corporate or governmental capture of the protocol.
The DAO exists to serve the network, not to profit from it.
Article II — Authority
The DAO governs:
protocol upgrades
incentive structures
role approvals
treasury allocations
emergency routing rules
dispute resolution
node standards
verification requirements
The DAO does not:
operate as a corporation
provide financial guarantees
act as a custodian
control individual nodes
interfere with free market activity
The DAO’s authority is limited to the protocol itself.
Article III — Membership
Membership is open to:
farmers
processors
refiners
warehouses
truckers
container haulers
auditors
brokers
developers
users
researchers
Membership is earned through:
participation
contribution
reputation
governance involvement
No individual or entity may hold more than 1% of total governance power without DAO approval.
Article IV — Governance
1. Voting
Votes are conducted on‑chain.
Each proposal must include:
purpose
rationale
implementation details
risks
timeline
2. Quorum
A minimum of 10% of active governance power is required for a vote to pass.
3. Proposal Types
Standard proposals
Emergency proposals
Upgrade proposals
Treasury proposals
Role approval proposals
4. Transparency
All proposals, votes, and outcomes are public and immutable.
Article V — Roles & Permissions
The DAO approves roles for:
farmers
processors
refiners
warehouses
transporters
auditors
brokers
Role approval requires:
identity verification (cryptographic or reputation‑based)
compliance with node standards
adherence to safety protocols
Roles may be revoked for:
fraud
repeated delivery failures
malicious activity
safety violations
Article VI — Treasury
The DAO treasury funds:
development
audits
grants
infrastructure
emergency incentives
routing subsidies
research
education
Treasury funds may not be used for:
personal enrichment
political activity
corporate lobbying
unrelated ventures
All treasury movements require DAO approval.
Article VII — Emergency Powers
In the event of:
supply chain collapse
regional instability
natural disaster
systemic failure
critical backlog of essential goods
The DAO may:
increase incentives
reroute deliveries
subsidize transport
prioritize essential commodities
activate emergency protocols
Emergency powers expire automatically after 14 days unless renewed.
Article VIII — Foundation
The DCSN Foundation exists to:
provide legal protection
interface with regulators
manage trademarks
sign contracts
receive grants
hire developers
maintain documentation
support the DAO
The Foundation does not control the protocol.
It executes the will of the DAO.
Article IX — Amendments
This Constitution may be amended by:
a 2/3 majority vote
a minimum quorum of 15%
a 7‑day review period
Amendments must preserve:
decentralization
neutrality
open access
resilience
censorship resistance
Article X — Dissolution
The DAO may dissolve only if:
the protocol is fully decentralized
the Foundation is no longer required
all treasury funds are allocated to public goods
Dissolution requires a 3/4 majority vote.
Ratification
This Constitution becomes effective upon approval by the founding members of the DCSN DAO.
