# DCSN Governance

This folder contains the core governance framework for the Decentralized Commodity Settlement Network (DCSN).

It is composed of:

- `dao-constitution.md` — On-chain governance, authority, and mission of the DCSN DAO.
- `foundation-charter.md` — Legal and operational framework of the DCSN Foundation.
- `governance-process.md` — How proposals are created, discussed, and executed.
- `roles-and-permissions.md` — Role definitions, approvals, and revocation rules.
- `emergency-protocols.md` — Rules and constraints for emergency powers.

The core principles of DCSN governance are:

- Decentralization  
- Neutrality  
- Transparency  
- Open access  
- Resilience  
- Censorship resistance  

The DAO governs the protocol.  
The Foundation executes the will of the DAO in the legal and operational world.
