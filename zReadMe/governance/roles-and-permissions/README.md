# DCSN Roles and Permissions

This document expands on Article V of the DAO Constitution and defines operational roles in the DCSN ecosystem.

---

## 1. Role Types

Core roles include:

- **Farmer** — Produces raw agricultural goods.  
- **Processor** — Transforms raw goods into refined or intermediate products.  
- **Refiner** — Handles advanced processing and refinement.  
- **Warehouse Operator** — Stores goods and provides inventory proofs.  
- **Transporter / Truck Driver / Container Hauler** — Moves goods between locations.  
- **Port Handler** — Manages port‑side logistics and transfers.  
- **Auditor** — Verifies deliveries, inventory, and compliance.  
- **Broker** — Facilitates matching between buyers and sellers.  
- **Developer** — Contributes to protocol and tooling.  

---

## 2. Role Approval

Role approval requires:

- **Identity verification**  
  - cryptographic identity, reputation, or other verifiable credentials  
- **Compliance with node standards**  
  - software requirements  
  - security practices  
  - uptime / reliability expectations  
- **Adherence to safety protocols**  
  - handling of goods  
  - regulatory compliance where applicable  

Approval is granted via DAO governance or delegated processes defined by the DAO.

---

## 3. Permissions

Roles may receive permissions such as:

- ability to post and accept loads  
- ability to issue delivery confirmations  
- ability to submit inventory proofs  
- ability to participate in specific markets (e.g., temperature‑controlled goods)  
- ability to act as an auditor or arbitrator  

Permissions are encoded at the protocol level where possible (smart contracts, role registries).

---

## 4. Revocation

Roles may be revoked for:

- fraud  
- repeated delivery failures  
- malicious activity  
- safety violations  
- falsified proofs or attestations  

Revocation process:

1. Incident is reported (by users, auditors, or automated systems).  
2. Evidence is collected (on‑chain data, logs, attestations).  
3. A role‑revocation proposal is submitted if needed.  
4. The DAO (or delegated body) votes to revoke or suspend the role.  

Reinstatement, if allowed, may require:

- probation period  
- additional verification  
- slashing or penalties (if defined by the protocol).

---

## 5. Reputation and Incentives

- Roles may be associated with reputation scores.  
- High‑reputation nodes may:
  - receive better incentives  
  - be prioritized for critical routes  
  - be eligible for specialized roles (e.g., emergency logistics).  

Reputation mechanisms should be transparent and, where possible, on‑chain or cryptographically verifiable.
